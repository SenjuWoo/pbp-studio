1 Describe your character

Two words gives you a generic knight. Say what it looks like: head shape, outfit, colours.

The app first draws one full-body design from your text (or uses your image), then redraws every template piece from it, so hair, colours and trim match in all 7 head angles, the bodies, giants, weapon, shields and portraits. The 15 beefy-form arms keep the template's own art. A full character takes about 4 minutes on Klein.
